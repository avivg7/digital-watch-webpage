**Status (2026-11-03):** the section plays **the owner's YouTube upload of the Channel 13 report** (`https://youtu.be/DBdZiXJb9OI`, video ID `DBdZiXJb9OI`) inside the page's own modal player. It replaced the first release's nine-second synthetic demo, which no longer exists in the tree, the build or the UI (it remains in git history, commit `d939f08`, together with the self-hosting scripts it used; nothing is wired to them).

## 2. What is built

- `src/components/FeaturedVideo.astro`, right after the hero (`#video`): the three-sentence headline (the last underlined in coral), the supporting copy, a 16:9 poster using the owner's supplied frame `video_front` (local files, **no YouTube thumbnail, no third-party request**; see section 2b), a blue Play disc with a white ring and soft glow exactly in the centre, and the label "לצפייה בתחקיר המלא" just below it. Under it: one honest line (the report is Channel 13's, shown by YouTube embed, loaded only on click, rights reserved to their owners) and a plain link "לצפייה ביוטיוב" to the same video.
- **On click:** a native `<dialog>` (modal) opens and ONE iframe is created: `https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/DBdZiXJb9OI?autoplay=1&rel=0&playsinline=1&hl=he&origin=<page origin>`, with an accurate `title`, `allow="autoplay; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture"`, `allowfullscreen` (fullscreen comes from this attribute; naming it in `allow` too makes Chromium warn), `referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin"`. No YouTube API script. The ID comes from `src/data/video.config.json`, never from the URL.
- **On close** (Escape, either close button, a click on the dim area): the iframe is set to `about:blank` and **removed**, so playback and audio stop and nothing stays connected to YouTube; focus returns to the Play button; opening again builds a fresh player.
- **Fullscreen:** YouTube's own button; the iframe has `allowfullscreen`. On desktop Chromium and Firefox the live test asserts that `document.fullscreenElement` becomes the iframe; on WebKit it was observed (recorded as a test annotation) but is not asserted, because WebKit does not reliably grant fullscreen under automation. **Phones: only the emulated profiles ran; a real iPhone/Android fullscreen was not tested** (iOS plays through its own native player).
- **Failure handling:** the page treats "offline when Play is pressed", "the browser refused the frame (CSP violation)" and "the frame did not finish loading in 15 s" as failures and shows a `role="alert"` panel with "לצפייה ביוטיוב" (same ID) and a retry. It never substitutes anything else.
- **No JavaScript:** the Play button is hidden and a `<noscript>` link points to the same YouTube video.
- **Keyboard:** the first focus in the dialog is the close button. **Known limit:** while the keyboard focus is _inside_ the YouTube player, Escape belongs to YouTube (it exits fullscreen) and does **not** close our dialog (manual probe on Chrome, Firefox and WebKit with a real player; not a repository test). Way out: a second close button, "סגירת הנגן", sits after the player in the tab order; in the real player it is reached by **Tab (22 presses in Chromium and Firefox: YouTube has many controls)** or by Shift+Tab (3 presses in headed Chrome, a manual probe; in Firefox and WebKit 8 Shift+Tab presses did not reach it). Only the forward-Tab route is a repository test (`youtube-live.spec.ts`, Chromium and Firefox); Safari-family engines do not Tab onto buttons by default and were not exercised. A mouse or touch user is not affected.
- **Poster:** see 2b.

## 2b. The poster picture (`video_front`)

- **Source file:** `assets/images/video_front.png` (git-ignored original: 3448x1904 PNG, 5.2 MB, with EXIF/ICC metadata). It is a frame of the Channel 13 report: the Channel 13 logo, the lower-third ticker and subtitle, a video-chat window with a soldier whose face is blurred (by the broadcaster) and two women whose faces are **not** blurred.
- **Build:** `npm run assets:poster` (`scripts/build-video-poster.mjs`) cover-crops it to exactly 16:9 (a few percent of the width; the picture is not otherwise edited: no blur, no retouch), resizes to 640/1200/1920 px and writes `public/img/video-front-{640,1200,1920}.{avif,webp}` (16 to 75 KB each, metadata stripped; the 5 MB original is never published). The publication gate's image checks (size, no metadata) apply.
- **Behaviour:** the poster is one `<button>`. Inside: a `<picture>` (AVIF, WebP; `loading="lazy"`, explicit 1920x1080 so the 16:9 box never shifts), a navy veil for legibility, the blue disc (brand gradient #0474fc to #0a58ca, white ring, cyan glow) centred by `inset:0; margin:auto` (tested to within 1.5 px at six widths), and the Hebrew label below it. The picture is decorative for assistive technology (`alt=""`, `aria-hidden`); the button's name is its visible text "לצפייה בתחקיר המלא" plus screen-reader-only text: "סרטון מיוטיוב, נפתח בחלון נגן" and a description of the picture. Hover: the disc grows and glows more, the picture eases in 3% and the veil lightens (all off with reduced motion, the OS or the panel). High-contrast mode: black disc, yellow ring and triangle, black label.
- **Rights and privacy (the owner decided to use it):** the poster is a **frame of a third-party broadcast showing two unblurred, identifiable people, visible on the public page without any click and stored in this public repository's history** (`public/img/video-front-*`). This goes further than the embed, which only shows them after a click. The earlier statement "no frame of the report is in this repository" is **no longer true**. Nothing here is a clearance (V-1).
- **Nothing is hidden behind an iframe:** before the click there is no iframe at all; the poster is the only thing the visitor sees. On click the player opens in the modal as before; the poster stays on the page behind it.

## 3. Privacy and security changes

- **CSP** (`src/layouts/Base.astro`): added `frame-src https://www.youtube-nocookie.com` and nothing else. No wildcard, no `script-src`/`img-src`/`connect-src` change; the regular `youtube.com` domain and any other origin stay blocked (tested with real violations). The `frame-src` directive covers only the iframe itself; the embedded document runs under YouTube's own policy.
- **Before Play:** zero requests to any other origin (tested on every route and again for the section; the Lighthouse run shows 0 third-party requests). After Play the browser contacted `www.youtube-nocookie.com`, `www.google.com`, `*.googlevideo.com`, `fonts.gstatic.com`, `i.ytimg.com`, `www.gstatic.com`, `yt3.ggpht.com` and `jnn-pa.googleapis.com` (measured in Chromium).
- **`/privacy/`** now has a section "הסרטון המוטמע מיוטיוב": nothing connects until Play; then the browser talks to Google/YouTube directly (IP, browser details, the site's origin); privacy-enhanced mode reduces personalisation but **does not mean no data is sent**; YouTube may store cookies or local data; closing the player removes it; the alternative is not pressing Play; link to Google's privacy policy; **no legal-compliance claim**. The "no cookies / no trackers" statements are now qualified accordingly. `/legal/` and `/accessibility/` were corrected (the report is no longer "not hosted/linked only": it is embedded; the player's accessibility is YouTube's, and captions were not checked).
- **Referrer policy:** unchanged site-wide `strict-origin-when-cross-origin`: YouTube receives the origin only, no path or query.
- The publication gate (`scripts/verify-public-tree.mjs`) now allows **no video or audio file at all**, accepts only the configured video ID (and fails on any other YouTube ID, `ytimg`, `googlevideo`, the IFrame API script, a regular-domain embed host, a mismatched watch URL), and still fails on a file identical to anything in `assets/videos/`, a local path or a name leak. The gate also checks the CSP meta tag of every page (exactly one third-party origin, `frame-src` for it, no wildcard) and other YouTube URL shapes (`shorts/`, `live/`, `v/`, `youtu.be/`). Planted-violation tests in `tests/video-gate.spec.ts`.

## 4. Rights: unresolved, deliberately not claimed

The video is a **third-party TV broadcast** (Channel 13 logo; a video-chat window with **identifiable people, one blurred and others not**). Hosting it on the owner's own YouTube channel does **not** show that the owner may republish it, and embedding it here is a further act of publication on a site the owner intends to make public. The site makes no clearance or endorsement claim ("הזכויות בו שמורות לבעליהן"; `/legal/` says embedding is not evidence of approval). Status `EMBEDDED_THIRD_PARTY_RIGHTS_UNVERIFIED` in `RIGHTS_AND_PUBLICATION_GATE.md` (R-1). This did not block development. **It should be resolved with the rights holder (and, because of the unblurred faces, with counsel) before the site is public.** The source MP4 was never read by the build, committed, transcoded or sampled; **the poster frame `video_front` is in this repository (see 2b); the dialog screenshots still show the test double, but the section screenshots now show the poster.**
