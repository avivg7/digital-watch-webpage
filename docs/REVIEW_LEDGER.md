# Review ledger

**These are AI reviews** (separate model sessions given the code and asked for defects). They are **not** a human audit, a legal opinion, a penetration test, or an accessibility audit with assistive technology. Round 1: three reviewers in parallel (product/storytelling/copy; visual/UX/accessibility; security/privacy/rights/deployment). Round 2: one fresh reviewer on the changed state (section 4). Dispositions: **FIXED**, **DECLINED** (with the reason), **OWNER** (needs the owner), **OPEN**.
