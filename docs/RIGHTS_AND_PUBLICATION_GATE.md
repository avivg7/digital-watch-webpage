# Rights and publication gate, and the owner approval checklist

This repository is **public** (GitHub Pages needs a public repository on the free plan), so everything committed here is public. The gate below decides what may be committed or deployed. The originals live in `assets/`, which is git-ignored, and `scripts/verify-public-tree.mjs` fails the build if anything outside the allow-list reaches the output.

Status words: `PUBLISHED_APPROVED` (a clear basis to publish), `PUBLISHED_OWNER_SUPPLIED` (the owner supplied and asked for it; a confirmation is still on the checklist), `PUBLISHED_REDACTED`, `LINKED_ONLY_RIGHTS_PENDING`, `NOT_PUBLISHED`.

## What is in the repository and what is not

- In: source, tests, scripts, docs, committed brand copies (`src/assets/brand`), and the **derived** images in `public/` (resized, re-encoded, metadata-free).
- Not in: `assets/` (originals, the video), the old prototype, any `.env`, any key, anything from the Digital Watch application's code, database or documents. The app was opened **read-only** for two brand files and its colour tokens.
- Commits use the GitHub no-reply identity, so the owner's personal e-mail address is **not** in the public history.

## Hold-back switches and the decisions taken after the independent review

The security/rights reviewer recommended holding back the portraits, the CIC logo and the "במסגרת CIC" statement, and not using the exact organisation credit, until written permission exists (items O-1 to O-5). **The owner's brief explicitly asked for these items** (both portraits, both logos, the CIC association, the exact copyright line) and said to record a missing confirmation here rather than omit them, so they are **published with their open items listed above**. That is a decision for the owner to confirm or reverse:

- `src/data/site.ts` has `SHOW = { portraits: true, cicLogo: true }`. Setting either to `false` and redeploying removes it from every page (portraits become text-only profiles; the CIC card loses its logo). It is a one-line change.
- The organisation name is one constant (`SITE.orgNameHe`), used in the footer, the legal page and Omri's title.
- If any of O-1 to O-5 is answered "no", flip the switch and redeploy the same day.

## About this document being public

The repository is public, so this checklist is public. It lists what is **not yet confirmed**; it makes no accusation about any source. Items stay here, visibly open, until the owner closes them. If the owner prefers that rights correspondence stay private, move this file and the audit to a private location and keep a one-paragraph summary here.

The screenshot's own badge ("דוגמה סינתטית — לא אדם אמיתי") is the product's label, shown inside the image; it is not a statement made by this site, and the site no longer repeats it in the caption.
